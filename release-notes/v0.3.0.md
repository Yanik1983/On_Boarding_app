## What's new in v0.3.0

**Much sharper and more detailed 3D**
- The 3D view now renders at the screen's full resolution on every device, including phones (up to 3×). Standard 1080p screens are supersampled at 1.5× for crisper edges and text. Earlier versions could render phones at half resolution.
- Ambient occlusion (soft contact shading), sharper shadows and reflections, and polished and brushed-metal materials give every object depth and definition.
- More detailed models: bevelled station platforms with chrome trim; a heart with blood vessels and coronary arteries; a Credo monolith with a frame and stakeholder symbols; a globe-style business-unit hub; and a campus with glass windows, rooftop units, a parking lot with cars, trees and street lamps. Products are more detailed too: the mapping system shows a colour-coded heart map, and catheters, pump, staplers, energy device and robot have visible parts.
- 3D labels are crisper, with a white outline so they stay readable over the scene. Distant fog no longer washes out the station you are looking at.
- Menus and panels: pixel-exact positioning (no blurry text), more opaque panels and darker, slightly larger text.

**Also included (since v0.1.0)**
- No quick-check question after each station: a station unlocks the next once all its pages have been viewed. The final Knowledge Check and certificate are unchanged.
- *Back* on a station's first page returns to the previous station.
- Phones: the 3D sits above the text, and *Hide text* gives the 3D the whole screen.

## How to use it

1. Download **`JnJ-MedTech-Onboarding-v0.3.0.html`** below (under *Assets*).
2. Double-click it. It opens in Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome, with no installation and no internet needed.
3. To share it, copy it to a network drive or email it **zipped** (many mail filters block `.html` attachments).

If an older computer feels slow, open Settings (gear icon) and choose **Auto** quality.

The content is still a **draft**: fill in the Our Site placeholders, paste the official Credo text, and have it reviewed before rollout.
