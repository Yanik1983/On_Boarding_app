## What's new in v0.4.0

**Smoother on computers**
- Every station is prepared when the app starts, so flying from station to station no longer stalls.
- Station flights are now cinematic: the camera eases in, glides along a gentle arc and settles, instead of jumping off at full speed.
- Lighter rendering with the same look: no costly "glass" pass, half-resolution contact shading, smaller shadow maps and no background blur behind the panels.
- Automatic smoothness: if a computer struggles, the most expensive effects are switched off first and resolution is lowered only as a last resort. Computers with spare power get extra sharpness.

**Livelier page changes**
- Pages slide in from the direction you move, their parts appear one after another, and the title's accent line draws in.
- The page progress bar fills with a small animation, and a page counter (e.g. 2 / 4) shows where you are.
- The camera turns to a new angle on every page, so each Next feels like a step in 3D.

**Your name, everywhere**
- The name you enter appears straight away in the top bar (initials badge and first name) and in the 3D welcome, which updates as you type.
- Starting the journey shows a personal welcome message, and some cards greet you by name.

**Simpler start**
- The Welcome page shows only *Start my journey*; the extra "Enter your name to start" button is gone.

## How to use it

1. Download **`JnJ-MedTech-Onboarding-v0.4.0.html`** below (under *Assets*).
2. Double-click it. It opens in Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome, with no installation and no internet needed.
3. To share it, copy it to a network drive or email it **zipped** (many mail filters block `.html` attachments).
