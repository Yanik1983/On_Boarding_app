## What's new in v0.4.1 – much smoother

**Stutter at every station change removed**
- The studio lighting was being recalculated each time you moved to a station, a heavy job that froze the picture at the start of every flight. It is now calculated once.
- The letter shapes for all 3D labels are prepared at startup, so labels no longer re-lay themselves out mid-journey.
- Nothing is rebuilt during flights any more; lines and shapes are created once.

**Lighter graphics that stay stable**
- New quality levels: **Auto** (default) checks your computer during the first seconds and picks **Balanced** or **Smooth**, then keeps that setting. Constantly switching effects was itself causing stutters.
- *Smooth* draws the 3D directly, with no post-processing, the lightest possible path. *Balanced* uses cheaper anti-aliasing and half-resolution depth shading. *Ultra 4K* is unchanged.
- Large surfaces use lighter shading, and an extra light was removed.

**Help when a computer has no graphics acceleration**
- Some company laptops, remote desktops and virtual machines run the browser without graphics acceleration, which makes any 3D slow. The app now detects this, starts in its lightest mode and explains how to turn acceleration on (Edge/Chrome: Settings › System › *Use graphics acceleration when available*), or offers text-only mode.
- Settings shows the quality level in use, the frame rate and the graphics chip, which helps when checking a slow computer.

**Offline guarantee kept**
- 3D labels only use characters from the built-in font, so the app never downloads fonts from the internet. Names in other alphabets still appear in the panels and on the certificate.

## How to use it

1. Download **`JnJ-MedTech-Onboarding-v0.4.1.html`** below (under *Assets*).
2. Double-click it. It opens in Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome, with no installation and no internet needed.
3. To share it, copy it to a network drive or email it **zipped** (many mail filters block `.html` attachments).
