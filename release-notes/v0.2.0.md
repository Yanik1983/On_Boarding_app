## What's new in v0.2.0

- **No more quick-check questions after each station.** A station now unlocks the next one once all its pages have been viewed. The final Knowledge Check quiz and certificate stay as before.
- Stations can still get an optional quick question later: add a `"checkpoint"` to that station in the editable content (see the README).

## How to use it

1. Download **`JnJ-MedTech-Onboarding-v0.2.0.html`** below (under *Assets*).
2. Double-click it. It opens in Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome, with no installation and no internet needed.
3. To share it, copy it to a network drive or email it **zipped** (many mail filters block `.html` attachments).

The content is still a **draft**: fill in the Our Site placeholders, paste the official Credo text, and have it reviewed before rollout.
