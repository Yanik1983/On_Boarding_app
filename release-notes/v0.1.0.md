First version of the J&J MedTech New Employee Journey: a 3D onboarding experience in **one HTML file**.

## How to use it

1. Download **`JnJ-MedTech-Onboarding-v0.1.0.html`** below (under *Assets*).
2. Double-click it. It opens in Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome, with no installation and no internet needed.
3. To share it, copy it to a network drive or email it **zipped** (many mail filters block `.html` attachments).

## What's inside

- Nine stations along a 3D path: Welcome, Our Company, Our Credo, Our Business Units, How We're Organised, Technology & Science, Our Products, Our Site, and a Knowledge Check.
- Interactive 3D at every station. The Technology lab has four demos: lens optics, heart rhythm and ablation, a knee replacement, and a product's lifecycle.
- Each station unlocks when you answer its quick question. The quiz at the end gives a printable certificate.
- Works on desktops, laptops, 4K screens (Ultra 4K quality setting) and phones (the 3D sits above the text, which you can hide).
- Progress is saved in the browser on that computer. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- Text-only mode for slower computers and screen readers, and reduced motion.
